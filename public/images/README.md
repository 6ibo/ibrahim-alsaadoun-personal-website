# Ibrahim's photographs

`channel-logo.jpg` is used in the hero and navigation; the interface crops the source's outer black corners and keeps the ivory logo field. `gallery/portrait-green-cutout.png` is the About portrait. The remaining supplied gallery photographs live in `gallery/`; all cards use the same 4:5 display frame with the site's warm neutral background. `content-algeria.jpg` and `content-football.jpg` remain the two video covers and are not shown in the personal photo gallery.

To add gallery photos, copy compressed images into this folder and add their paths and translated labels to the `photos` list in `src/data/content.ts`. The source photo that arrived as a completely black HEIC was left out until a readable copy is available. No stock portrait is used in the site.
